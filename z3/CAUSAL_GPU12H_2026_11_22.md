## Interpretation

The model is still improving, but the slope collapsed. The 955M-token corpus
is not the limiter: 175.8M tokens is only 18% of it. The limiter is the
single-T4 training budget and, increasingly, model capacity/optimization.
A third identical 6-hour block would likely buy roughly 0.01–0.02 PTB. A
larger or faster accelerator (TPU v5e-8, Blackwell) is the lever that matters
now, not more 6-hour T4 chunks.
