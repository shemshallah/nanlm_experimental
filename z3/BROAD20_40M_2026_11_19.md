# Broad non-WikiText 40-minute run (20 + 20, 2026-11-19)

Fresh 115,238,237-parameter Z4 model trained from scratch for 20 minutes,
then continued for a second 20 minutes from the saved checkpoint. No
WikiText or Wikipedia collection in the corpus. This is the first
checkpoint of the current session that was persisted to Kaggle.

Numerics: bf16 autocast (no scaler), TF32, fused AdamW, RMSNorm in fp32,
no gradient checkpointing, dense MoE dispatch.

The second stage resumed at step 700 and ran a fresh WSD horizon of 1400
steps, so the LR stayed at 4e-4 through the stable phase and decayed over
the last ~140 steps. Training loss fell from 10.82 at step 10 to **1.34**
at step 1360.

## Corpus

291 MB / 432,947 documents, chunked to ~1 KB to avoid the tokenizer's
superlinear long-document cost. Documents were shuffled before splitting.

- TinyStories (GPT-4 validation split)
- AG News train
- FineWeb sample, first 70 MB of text
- 13 NaNROUTER domain releases: news, Q&A, recipes, code, math, arXiv,
  PubMed, law, patents, standards, security, chemistry, finance
- English word and spelling lists

| Split | Tokens | Documents |
|---|---:|---:|
| train | 75,154,683 | 422,253 |
| valid | 760,130 | 4,200 |
| test | 773,900 | 4,333 |

Valid/test are chunk-level splits of the same sources, so **in-domain BPB
is optimistic**: neighbouring chunks of the same document can land in
different splits.

PTB BPB **2.2033 → 1.3063**, top-1 **14.17% → 35.36%**. The lost 55-minute
checkpoint reached 0.8679 BPB / 50.84% on the same file, so the 40-minute
model is still short of it despite the steep early curve.

The in-domain drop from 1.43 to 0.44 is partly real learning and partly
chunk leakage; do not compare it across corpora.

## What this says about the 7-hour run

The 20→40 minute PTB curve is still steep, so token budget matters more
than any single architecture tweak. At the current ~20k tok/s, 7 hours is
only ~500M tokens; with the Blackwell fixes (sparse MoE dispatch, causal
attention without a 512×512 mask, GQA without KV expansion, CPU sidecar
prefetch, `torch.compile`) 2–3× is plausible, giving 1.0–1.5B tokens.

A floor-limited extrapolation puts PTB at roughly **0.75–0.90 BPB** for
1–2B tokens, with a wide error bar; the early curve cannot be extrapolated
linearly because it would imply sub-SOTA PTB. The larger, more reliable
win will be generation fluency and in-domain structure.

The corpus for that run must be built first: 1B tokens ≈ 3.8 GB of text,
and the current single-process tokenizer runs at ~0.44 MB/s (~2.4 hours).
Parallel tokenization across the 20 available CPU cores is the first
practical step.
