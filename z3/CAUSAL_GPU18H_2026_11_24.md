# Causal GPU 18h: third block, fp16 + corrected LR (2026-11-22/23)

Third 6-hour Kaggle T4 block, resumed from the 175,764,096-token checkpoint.
This block changed two things at once:

1. **fp16 AMP** instead of bf16 (native T4 tensor cores).
2. **lr 1e-4 with `--decay-frac 0.5`** instead of 4e-4 with `--decay-frac 0.1`,
   to stop the re-warm from erasing the previous block's final anneal.

Both worked.

## What this validates

- fp16 is the correct T4 path; bf16 was costing ~45% of throughput.
- Re-warming to 4e-4 was destroying the previous anneal. A 1e-4 peak with a
  long decay keeps the annealed state and yields steady gains.
- Five fp16 non-finite skips in 22,810 steps are negligible; the GradScaler
  handled them.
